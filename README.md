# TeraFarm : Cultivating Urban Agriculture through Collaborative Growth and AI-Powered Guidance

### By Manish M, Jyoti Prakash Behera

## Problem Statement
In this new age, good, sustainably-grown, healthy food is gaining popularity. But, with growing cities, the land to grow and support our population with quality food is challenging. TeraFarm addresses the growing need for sustainable urban agriculture by empowering apartment dwellers to cultivate their own food, fostering community collaboration, and promoting environmental awareness. 

## Proposed Solution
- TeraFarm eliminates the barriers to urban farming by providing a user-friendly mobile application that facilitates small-scale vegetable cultivation on balconies and terraces, and aids selling the excess good-quality, organic produce for a small profit.
- TeraFarm aims to create a significant positive impact by increasing access to fresh produce, promoting community engagement, and enhancing urban greenery.

## Unique Selling Proposition
- Recognizing the challenge of selling limited produce, TeraFarm introduces a collaborative marketplace feature, enabling neighbors to pool their harvests and sell collectively within their neighborhood, creating a viable side hustle with minimal investment.
- The core innovation lies in the integration of 3 AI-driven personalized guidance systems: TeraDoc, TeraRecommend and TeraCare.
- Leveraging the Gemini API and Tensorflow's Keras ML Model, TeraFarm offers a custom chatbot TeraCare and TeraRecommend that adapts to individual space, preferences, and experience levels. By integrating the OpenWeather API, the chatbot provides real-time, location-specific recommendations on optimal vegetable selection and growth tips based on weather and geographic conditions.
- TeraDoc Image Analyser analyses plant health based on uploaded pictures. Based on identified potential diseases, AI recommendations are made for aiding plant wellbeing.
- Gamification of Leaderboard and Badges encourages users to complete daily tasks and take care of their plant buddies, providing them with rewards in the form of coupons and XP. Custom icons make the UI more appealing and unique, giving the app a recognisable feel.
- Integration of Shop features to promote nearby nurseries and plant essential providers, encouraging sales for enthusiastic users via coupons and promotions, helps boost their business and makes it convenient for the users, making TeraFarm a one-stop application.

## Future Plans 
- Implementation in multiple Regional Languages to reach a wider range of audiences.
- Text-to-speech and speech-to-text options to enhance accessibility.
- Web scraping techniques are employed to deliver up-to-date information on sustainable agricultural practices, empowering users to adopt eco-friendly methods.
- Automatic tracking and removal of old / expired produce based on post date to ensure quality of product.
- Dynamic forum-like feed for users to share experience-based video tutorials and suggestions, fostering a thriving community of urban growers. This crowdsourced knowledge base, similar to an Instagram feed tailored for urban agriculture, will empower users and accelerate the adoption of sustainable urban farming practices.
